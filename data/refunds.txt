Demo company policy — Refunds (fictional, for learning only)
A customer can request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery if the item is unused. After a returned package is received and inspected, eligible refunds are initiated within 5 business days. The bank may take an additional 3 to 7 business days to display the refund. For an incorrect refund amount, contact customer support with the order number. This policy is a synthetic example and is not legal or financial advice.
